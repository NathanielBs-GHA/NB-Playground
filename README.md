# NB-Playground
# 📊 Passion Projects & Analytics Lab

Welcome to my personal playground of GitHub! This repository serves as a centralized hub for my data experiments, predictive models, and deep-dive analytics. I use this space to bridge the gap between complex raw data and actionable insights across three very different domains.

---

## 🏈 NFL Analytics
*Exploring the gridiron through a lens of efficiency and probability.*
- **Advanced Scouting:** Building models to track WR separation metrics and QB pocket composure.
- **Predictive Betting:** Using historical weather data and EPA (Expected Points Added) to forecast Over/Under trends.
- **Draft Logic:** A "best-available" algorithm that weights collegiate production against athletic combines.

## 📈 Stock Trading & Quant Finance
*Algorithmic approaches to the market and risk management.*
- **Momentum Scanners:** Python-based scrapers that identify relative strength in the S&P 500.
- **Backtesting Suite:** A custom environment for testing Mean Reversion strategies using `Pandas` and `Backtrader`.
- **Sentiment Analysis:** Real-time processing of financial news and social trends to gauge market "heat."

## 🦅 Migratory Bird Statistics
*Tracking the flight paths and population health of avian travelers.*
- **Migration Mapping:** Visualizing seasonal displacement patterns using GIS and public tracking data.
- **Phenology Trends:** Analyzing how shifting climates impact the timing of arrival for songbirds and raptors.
- **Population Density:** Using R to identify critical stopover habitats that require conservation focus.

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack
- **Languages:** Python (Pandas, Scikit-learn, NumPy), SQL, R.
- **Visuals:** Tableau, Matplotlib, Plotly.
- **Tools:** dbt core, Jupyter Notebooks, AWS (S3/Lambda), Git.
